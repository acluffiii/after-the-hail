What this is

A small, sourced guide for the questions owners actually ask: Should I file a claim? Do I have to use the “preferred” shop? What is PDR, and when is a $0 deductible offer a warning sign?

What this is not

It is not legal or insurance advice. It does not invent businesses, prices, or statistics. The directory is empty until a shop can be verified from a public source we open ourselves.

Why Texas, and why hail

The same questions spike after every storm in Houston, Dallas–Fort Worth, and San Antonio. Texas also has clear, citable rules: your insurer can recommend a shop, but it cannot require a preferred shop or suggest that coverage depends on using one. Those rules come from the Texas Department of Insurance and the Insurance Code, not from us.
