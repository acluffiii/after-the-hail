What we already know from opened sources

In May 2026, a widely discussed r/houston thread described golf-ball hail in Pearland and the usual after-storm crush: vehicles dented, roofers at the door, and questions about claims and premiums. Those comments are anecdotes, not a shop list and not official insurance rules.

The Texas rules that do apply in Houston — you may choose your repair shop, an insurer may recommend a preferred list but may not require it, comprehensive coverage can apply to hail if you bought it, and a deductible is owed on your own comprehensive claim — are cited in the statewide guide from TDI and the Insurance Code.
