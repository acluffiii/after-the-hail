How a shop will get listed

  • A public business website, a public maps listing, or a license/registration page is opened and saved as a source.
  • We record the name, city, and source URL, plus the date we opened it.
  • We do not invent star ratings, “best of,” or review counts.
  • A Reddit or X recommendation alone is not enough.

Shops are not ranked by paid placement, and a listing is not for sale.
