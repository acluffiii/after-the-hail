About

A guide with sources, not invented listings.

After the Hail is a Texas hail-repair and paintless dent repair guide. It is built to be useful and carefully sourced, not to sell shop listings.

Who’s behind this

This site is made by the team behind the DentDOG PDR estimator app. After the Hail is a separate, independent editorial guide: shops are not ranked by paid placement, and a listing here is not for sale. DentDOG may be mentioned later on technician-facing pages where an estimating or photo workflow is actually relevant. It is not an insurance company or a repair shop.

How we work

  • Every factual claim on a guide page should cite a URL we actually opened.
  • We do not invent businesses, reviews, prices, or statistics.
  • Owner and technician quotes from Reddit or X are anecdotes. They are labeled that way and are not official rules.
  • Directory listings stay empty until a shop can be verified from a public source such as the business’s own website or a public maps or license page.
  • Shops are not ranked by paid placement.

Affiliate disclosure

There are no affiliate or referral links on this site right now. If we add any later, we will disclose them clearly. A later affiliate link would mean we could earn a commission if you buy through it, at no extra cost to you. Those links will not change a factual claim, and they will not buy a shop a higher ranking or a directory listing.

What we are not

This site is not legal advice, insurance advice, or a promise about your claim, your rates, or your car. Policies, deductibles, and state rules differ. If you have a dispute, TDI’s consumer line is listed on TDI’s auto insurance guide as 800-252-3439.

After the Hail is a hail-repair and paintless dent repair guide for Texas car owners. It is not legal, insurance, or repair advice. Read your own policy, and confirm rules with the Texas Department of Insurance or a licensed professional.
